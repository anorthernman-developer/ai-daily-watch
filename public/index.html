  3. AI trendsMeta

    UK CMA investigates Meta’s commercial terms for using publishers’ content in AI products

    The UK CMA opened an investigation into whether Meta’s commercial terms for businesses and publishers could breach competition law by allowing Meta to collect and use their website content in its products, including AI features. The regulator has not made a finding that Meta broke the law; it will gather evidence from Meta and affected third parties.

  4. CopilotMicrosoft

    Microsoft expands Copilot with finance skills and partner-built plugins

    Microsoft announced more than 25 finance skills and partner-built plugins for accounting, capital markets, and other finance work. Accounting capabilities in Copilot in Excel and Cowork are available in Frontier, while the Capital Markets and Finance plugins for Cowork are generally available; Dynamics 365 ERP and partner plugins are beginning to roll out.

    feature update · Published 29 Oct 2026 · Briefing 31 Oct 2026 · microsoft.com

  5. Windows & AutopilotMicrosoft

    Microsoft Intune October updates add security baselines, EPM network controls, and Autopatch scheduling

    Microsoft’s October Intune updates add a Windows 365 for Agents security baseline and let standard users make IT-approved network-setting changes through Endpoint Privilege Management without broad admin rights. Windows Autopatch also gains expanded approval and scheduling controls for quality updates, including independent management of different update types.

    feature update · Published 29 Oct 2026 · Briefing 31 Oct 2026 · techcommunity.microsoft.com

  6. Windows & AutopilotMicrosoft

    Microsoft updates KB5127936 notes with modernized AutoPlay details and a Windows 11 24H2 update clarification

    Microsoft’s KB5127936 notes were updated on October 29 to detail a modernized AutoPlay experience, including dark mode, improved text scaling, and notifications in Notification Center. The notes also clarify that Windows 11 24H2 no longer receives non-security preview updates starting with the October 2026 release; Enterprise and Education editions continue to receive monthly security updates.

    feature update · Published 29 Oct 2026 · Briefing 31 Oct 2026 · support.microsoft.com

  7. Windows & AutopilotMicrosoft

    Microsoft Edge adds accelerated app launches on Windows 11

    Microsoft announced that Edge is the first browser to support Windows accelerated app launches, with a stated 15% launch-speed improvement for most users and up to 33% under heavy multitasking. The capability is supported with Edge 150 or later on Windows 11 versions 25H2, 26H2, and 26H1 when KB5127936 or a later update is installed.

    feature update · Published 29 Oct 2026 · Briefing 31 Oct 2026 · blogs.windows.com

    Microsoft released Visual Studio 2026 version 18.11.3 on October 29 with security advisory fixes. The listed issues include a libcurl HTTP/2 upload use-after-free, a Brotli decoder out-of-bounds write, and a Visual Studio certificate-validation vulnerability.

  10. Code & GitHubMicrosoft

    Microsoft details vcpkg’s October update, including configurable cache compression

    Microsoft summarized October changes to vcpkg, including a configurable binary-cache compression level, improved cache diagnostics and reliability, and updated ports such as Boost 1.92.0. The compression option lets developers trade ZIP archive size against cache submission time.

    feature update · Published 29 Oct 2026 · Briefing 31 Oct 2026 · devblogs.microsoft.com

  11. Anthropic

    Anthropic describes Claude’s self-assessment system for flagging potential misuse

    Anthropic publicly described Claude’s “self-assessment” mechanism, which produces a preliminary safety assessment after a response and can flag potential misuse. The company said it had used the technique since June and that it had surfaced misuse not caught by other safeguards; it is described as one safety signal, not as a replacement for broader safeguards.

    policy/safety · Published 29 Oct 2026 · Briefing 31 Oct 2026 · x.com

  12. Anthropic

    Claude API request-size limit increases from 32 MB to 128 MB

    Anthropic increased the first-party Claude API’s maximum request size for Messages and Token Counting from 32 MB to 128 MB, giving image-heavy requests such as large PDFs and computer-use sessions more headroom. For requests with more than 20 images, it also raised the per-image limit from 2,000 to 3,000 pixels; cloud-hosted platforms have separate limits.

    feature update · Published 29 Oct 2026 · Briefing 31 Oct 2026 · x.com
