  2. xAI and OpenAI

    Study reports partisan differences in forced candidate recommendations by Grok and OpenAI models

    A Convovo study reported that, when explicitly forced to recommend a candidate across nearly 500 U.S. midterm races, Grok 4.7 chose Republicans in 92% of its answers and OpenAI’s GPT-6.1 Sol chose Democrats in 97%. The findings raise questions about model behavior and political neutrality, but the article cautions that the test used APIs and an unusually direct prompt, not ordinary chatbot use.
